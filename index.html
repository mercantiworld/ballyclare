Ballyclare is a modern healthcare application designed to simplify the way patients book appointments, healthcare teams manage consultations and medical centres handle payments.

Booking and payments

More Than Just Online Payments

Traditional booking systems often treat appointments and payments as separate processes.

Ballyclare brings them together.

01

Booking online

Patients booking online can securely reserve an available appointment and complete payment before their booking is confirmed.

02

Booked by practice staff

For appointments arranged directly by practice staff, the system supports a more flexible approach: create the appointment, record the consultation charge and take payment immediately or leave an outstanding balance for later settlement.

Two booking journeys. One connected financial system.

Workflows

Designed for Real Healthcare Workflows

01

Secure Online Booking

Patients can discover available appointments, temporarily reserve their selected slot and complete payment through Stripe before their booking is finalised.

02

Flexible Practice Payments

Practice staff can book appointments without requiring immediate payment, ensuring that administrative or payment circumstances do not unnecessarily prevent a consultation from being arranged.

03

Outstanding Balance Management

Consultation charges, successful payments, partial payments and refunds are connected to the appointment, providing a clearer picture of what has been paid and what remains outstanding.

04

Patient Payment Visibility

The patient portal provides access to payment history, receipts and outstanding balances, with support for paying existing charges.

05

Reliable Payment Processing

The platform is designed to handle payment confirmations, failures, repeated provider notifications and refund updates without creating duplicate bookings or financial records.
